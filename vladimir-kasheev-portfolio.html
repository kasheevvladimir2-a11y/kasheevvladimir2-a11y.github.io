About

Vladimir Kasheev is an independent AI Concept Architect and Human-AI Collaboration Designer based in Brooklyn, New York. His work is built on one core conviction: AI should sharpen human intelligence, not replace it.

Across multiple frameworks — AriaDNA, Razum-Ka, and the Mifatoria Protocol — Vladimir has developed protocols and tools that put human reasoning at the center of every AI interaction, creating a genuine co-reasoning architecture rather than a passive assistant model.

His vision extends into education, climate resilience, and precision medicine — deploying the AIzia platform as an ethically governed intelligence system not tied to any single corporate provider.

Before his work in AI architecture, Vladimir spent over a decade as a certified Industrial Hygiene Inspector and Project Monitor in New York City, overseeing complex environmental abatement projects — grounding his approach to systems design in rigorous, real-world accountability.
